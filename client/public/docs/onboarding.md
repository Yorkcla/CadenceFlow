# Welcome to CadenceFlow 🌀

CadenceFlow helps you design interactive experiences through sound by leveraging musical principles of tension and release. It guides you through a structured, three-stage process:

**Storyboarding** – Define user scenarios and contexts where sound might enhance interaction.

**Sounding** – Assign sounds to key moments based on user cognitive flow.

**Reviewing** – Review the overall outputs, including storyboard visuals and sound assignments.

CadenceFlow also integrates **AI-assisted generation** of text, images, and videos to support creative exploration, especially for non-experts in music or design.

## Storyboarding 🖼️

The first stage focuses on mapping out the user interaction flow through visual storyboarding. You can organize your design into **Phases** and **Steps**, each with descriptions and representative images, optionally supported by GenAI suggestions.

### Phase

A phase represents a broader segment of the interaction, such as a scene or an overall interaction context. You can describe it with text, upload or draw an image, and generate visuals using AI.

![Phase Card](/docs/images/PhaseCard.jpg)

### Step

Each phase can be divided into smaller steps that capture specific actions or moments. Steps can include descriptions and visuals, either AI generated, user drawn, or uploaded, to construct a cohesive narrative flow.

![Step Card](/docs/images/StepCard.jpg)

---

## Sounding 🎧

In the second stage, you’ll assign sounds to key phases or steps, along with cognitive states, interaction intent, and urgency, supported by helpful AI suggestions. The AI considers musical tension and release to suggest fitting sound features. This process involves **Card Selection** for relevant user contexts and using a dedicated **Sound Panel** to configure sound details.

### Card Selection

Select one or more Phase or Step cards to start setting up their sounds. Fill in details like **Cognitive Phase**, **Interaction Intent**, and **Urgency** to help the AI suggest sounds that fit the moment.

![Card Selection](/docs/images/CardSelection.jpg)

### Sound Panel

Each selected card opens a sound panel below, where you can adjust settings such as **Pitch**, **Rhythm**, **Duration**, and **Timbre**. These parameters are initially filled with AI-generated suggestions tailored to the card, followed by recommendations that help shape overall **Musical Tension and Release** throughout the sound flow — that’s the idea behind CadenceFlow!

![Sound Panel](/docs/images/SoundPanel.jpg)

---

## Reviewing 🔍

The final stage allows you to **Preview and Compare** your interactive sound design outputs, with options to **Generate Videos**. You can view the complete visual narrative, hear synchronised sound cues, and create videos with sound using AI assistance.

### Preview and Compare

Review all assigned sounds and visuals in sequence to evaluate their combinations. You can also upload multiple projects for side-by-side comparison.

![Review Card](/docs/images/ReviewCard.jpg)

### Video Generation

Generate a video version of your storyboard with assigned sounds using GenAI to visualise the final experience.
