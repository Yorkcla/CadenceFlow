# Welcome to CadenceFlow

**CadenceFlow** helps you design interactive experiences through sound, using principles of **musical tension and release**.  
This tool guides you through a structured process with three stages: **Storyboarding**, **Sounding**, and **Reviewing**.  
It integrates AI-assisted generation of text, images, and videos to support creative exploration—especially for non-experts.

---

## 🎬 Storyboarding

The first stage involves defining the interaction flow of your design using visual storyboarding.  
You can organize the flow into **Phases** and **Steps**, each of which can include text, image, or AI-generated media.

### 🌀 Phase

A phase represents a broader segment of the interaction, such as a scene or system state.  
You can describe it with text, upload or draw an image, and optionally generate visuals using AI.

![Phase Example](./images/phase-example.png)

### 🪜 Step

Each phase can be broken down into smaller **steps**, describing detailed actions or moments.  
Steps can include AI-generated or user-drawn short videos that build the narrative flow.

![Step Example](./images/step-example.png)

---

## 🎧 Sounding

In the second stage, you assign sounds to each phase or step to convey intent, emotion, or urgency.  
This is done through **card selection** and the **sound panel** interface.

### 🃏 Card Selection

Select one or more phase/step cards to configure their associated sounds.  
You can assign parameters such as **cognitive phase**, **interaction intent**, and **urgency**.

![Card Selection](./images/card-selection.png)

### 🎛️ Sound Panel

Each selected card opens a **Sound Panel** below it, where you can edit sound settings like pitch, rhythm, tempo, and duration.  
AI suggestions based on tonal harmony and functional context are available to guide your choices.

![Sound Panel](./images/sound-panel.png)

---

## 📽️ Reviewing

The final stage lets you **preview and compare** your interactive sound design.  
You can view the full visual narrative and hear synchronized sound cues.

### ✅ Final Check

Review all assigned sounds and visuals in sequence to confirm their timing and effectiveness.

![Final Overview](./images/final-overview.png)

### 🎬 Video Generation

Optionally, generate a video version of your storyboard using GenAI to visualize the final experience.

![Generated Video](./images/video-generation.png)
