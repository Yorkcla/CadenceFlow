# Welcome to CadenceFlow

**CadenceFlow** helps you design interactive experiences through sound, using **musical tension and release**. This tool guides you through a structured process with three stages: **Storyboarding**, **Sounding**, and **Reviewing**. It integrates AI-assisted generation of text, images, and videos to support creative exploration for non-experts.

---

## Storyboarding

The first stage focuses on outlining your interaction flow through visual storyboarding. You can structure your design into **Phases** and **Steps**, each accompanied by descriptions and representative images, optionally supported from GenAI.

### Phase

A phase represents a broader segment of the interaction, such as a scene or system state. You can describe it with text, upload or draw an image, and generate visuals using AI.

![Phase Example](./images/phase-example.png)

### Step

Each phase can be divided into smaller steps that capture specific actions or moments. Steps may include descriptions and visuals, either AI generated, user drawn, or uploaded, to construct a cohesive narrative flow.

![Step Example](./images/step-example.png)

---

## Sounding

In the second stage, assign sounds to key phases or steps, along with cognitive states, interaction intent, urgency, and musical tension and release, with helpful AI support. This is done through **card selection** and a dedicated **sound panel** interface.

### Card Selection

Select one or more phase or step cards to configure their associated sounds. Define parameters like **cognitive phase**, **interaction intent**, and **urgency** to guide AI-assisted sound assignment.

![Card Selection](./images/card-selection.png)

### Sound Panel

Each selected card opens a sound panel below it, where you can adjust settings such as **pitch**, **rhythm**, **duration**, and **timbre**. AI suggestions are generated based on the parameters assigned to each card, along with follow-up recommendations that enhance overall **musical tension and release** throughout the sound flow.

![Sound Panel](./images/sound-panel.png)

---

## Reviewing

The final stage allows you to **preview and compare** your interactive sound design, with options to **generate videos**. You can view the complete visual narrative, hear synchronised sound cues, and create videos with sound using AI assistance. 

### Preview and Compare

Review all assigned sounds and visuals in sequence to evaluate their combinations. You can also upload multiple projects for side-by-side comparison.

![Final Overview](./images/final-overview.png)

### Video Generation

Optionally, generate a video version of your storyboard with assigned sounds using GenAI to visualise the final experience.

![Generated Video](./images/video-generation.png)
